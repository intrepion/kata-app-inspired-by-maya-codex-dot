KINFRAME / CHARACTER ANIMATION KATAReady when you are.

A little motion goes a long way.

Space: play/pause. K: set a full-pose key. Arrow keys: previous/next frame. Ctrl/⌘ Z: undo. Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z: redo. Ctrl/⌘ S: save.

Use the channel dropdown and slider for keyboard-accessible posing. Drag rig handles to rotate joints. Root movement uses its horizontal and lift sliders.

This original Maya-inspired kata focuses on 2D forward-kinematic posing and keyframe interpolation. It is not a 3D modeler: no inverse kinematics, skinned meshes, FBX, Maya files, or video export.

Autosave is best-effort. Download a project for a durable backup.
